3. Cookies and Browser Storage

Cookies are small files that websites may store in your browser. Third-party services used on a website may also use cookies or similar technologies.

Some PlayViral games may use browser storage, such as local storage, to save game preferences, scores or progress on your device. This information may remain in your browser until you clear it.

You can manage or delete cookies and browser storage through your browser settings. Disabling certain storage features may affect how parts of the website work.

We may use analytics services, such as Google Analytics, to understand website traffic, page views, visitor interactions and general usage patterns, if these services are enabled.

Analytics services may use cookies or similar technologies to collect information about website usage. Their processing of information is also governed by their respective privacy policies.

Emails and related information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, resolve issues or meet applicable legal obligations.
