Ball Sort Puzzle

Move the colored balls until every tube contains one color.

Score: 0
Ready when you are.

Tap one tube to pick its top ball, then tap another tube to move it. You can only place a ball on an empty tube or a matching color, with available space.

How to play Ball Sort Puzzle

Sort every color into its own tube in this free logic puzzle. Move one top ball at a time and use empty tubes to plan your sequence.

Tips

Use the empty tube as temporary storage. Avoid trapping a color beneath a different ball.
