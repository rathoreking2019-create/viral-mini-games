How to Play Ball Sort Puzzle

Ball Sort Puzzle is a color-organizing logic game. Your goal is to arrange the balls so that each tube contains balls of only one color. You need to think carefully about the order of your moves because moving one ball can affect the next few moves.

Game Instructions

  1. Select a tube containing balls to pick up its top ball.
  2. Select another tube to move the ball there.
  3. You can move a ball into an empty tube.
  4. You can also place a ball on a matching color if the tube has space.
  5. Continue until every color is organized into its own tube.

Ball Sort Puzzle Rules

The puzzle follows a simple set of sorting rules. You can move only the top ball from a tube. A ball can be placed in an empty tube or on top of a ball of the same color, provided there is enough room. Planning your moves is important because a tube can hold only a limited number of balls.

5 Tips to Solve Ball Sort Puzzles

1. Look at all the tubes first

Before making your first move, check which colors are already grouped together and which colors are blocking other balls.

2. Use empty tubes carefully

Empty tubes give you temporary space to move balls around. Avoid filling them without thinking about your next moves.

3. Complete matching colors when possible

Moving matching balls together can make the remaining puzzle easier to manage and reduce unnecessary moves.

4. Avoid blocking important colors

A ball placed above a different color can prevent you from accessing the balls underneath. Think about what each move will reveal or hide.

5. Plan more than one move ahead

Instead of moving balls randomly, consider how a move will affect the available space in the other tubes.

The objective is to organize the balls so that each tube contains balls of only one color.

You can place a ball on another ball only when the colors match, or move it into an empty tube, subject to available space.
