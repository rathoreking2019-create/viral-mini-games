How to Play Ball Sort Puzzle

Ball Sort Puzzle is a color-organizing logic game. Your goal is to arrange the balls so that each tube contains balls of only one color. Think carefully about the order of your moves because moving one ball can affect your next few moves.

Game Instructions

  1. Select a tube containing balls to pick up its top ball.
  2. Select another tube to move the ball there.
  3. You can move a ball into an empty tube.
  4. You can place a ball on a matching color if the tube has space.
  5. Continue until every color is organized into its own tube.

Ball Sort Puzzle Rules

You can move only the top ball from a tube. A ball can be placed in an empty tube or on top of a ball of the same color, provided there is enough room. Planning your moves is important because each tube has limited capacity.

Tips to Solve Ball Sort Puzzles

1. Look at all the tubes first

Check which colors are already grouped together and which colors are blocking other balls before making your first move.

2. Use empty tubes carefully

Empty tubes give you temporary space. Avoid filling them without thinking about your next moves.

3. Complete matching colors when possible

Moving matching balls together can make the remaining puzzle easier.

4. Avoid blocking important colors

A ball placed above a different color can prevent you from accessing the balls underneath.

5. Plan more than one move ahead

Consider how each move affects the available space in other tubes.
